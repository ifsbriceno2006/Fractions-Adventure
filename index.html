🍕 Module 1 · What is a fraction?

A fraction describes part of a whole. The whole is cut into equal parts.

🔼 Numerator (top): how many of those parts we are talking about.
🔽 Denominator (bottom): how many equal parts the whole is cut into.

🔁 Convert (mixed ⇄ improper)

Mixed → improper: whole × denominator + numerator, keep the denominator.
Improper → mixed: divide; the quotient is the whole, the remainder is the new numerator.

✖️ Module 4 · Multiply & divide

➡️ Multiply: numerator × numerator and denominator × denominator. Mixed numbers? Make them improper first!
➗ Divide: KEEP the first, CHANGE ÷ to ×, FLIP the second (swap the numerator and the denominator).
💡 Why flip? Dividing by 3/4 asks “how many 3/4’s fit?” — which is the same as multiplying by 4/3.
➖ Signs: multiplying same signs is positive; multiplying different signs is negative (dividing follows the same rule). Start with positives, then tick “neg”.

➕ Module 6 · Add & subtract

1️⃣ Same denominator
Add/subtract the numerators (tops), keep the denominator (bottom): 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8 (like adding slices of the same size).
2️⃣ Different denominators
Slices are different sizes! Rewrite with a common denominator (LCM is best), then combine the numerators (tops).
3️⃣ Negatives
Subtracting = adding the opposite: a − b = a + (−b). Signs go on the numerator: −3/4 = (−3)/4.
